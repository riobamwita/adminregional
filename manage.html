Current Hire Purchase Formula

Balance = Vehicle Price − Deposit

Monthly Principal = Balance ÷ Repayment Months

Facility Fee = KES 10,000 / month

Monthly Installment = Monthly Principal + KES 10,000

Balance KES 600,000
Period 6 months

Facility Fee KES 10,000
